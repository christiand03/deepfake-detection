# CLRP (Gu et al., 2018)

> [!info] Metadata
> **Authors:** Gu, Yang, Tresp
> **Year / Venue:** 2018 · ACCV (arXiv:1812.02100)
> **Evidence level:** abstract (2026-06-26)

## Project Relevance
**Direction source for our bivariate AttnLRP heatmap.** Standard single-target LRP is *not* class-discriminative; CLRP's target-minus-non-target contrast is exactly the `R_fake − R_real` decision margin whose sign drives the colour/hue channel of our heatmap (see [[../../Knowledge/AttnLRP Bivariate Heatmap]]). Establishes that "contrastive" is the principled fix for class-discriminative relevance.

## Summary
Shows LRP / Deep Taylor Decomposition explanations are not class-discriminative, and proposes **Contrastive LRP (CLRP)**: subtract the relevance of a contrastive (dual/non-target) concept to obtain instance-specific, class-discriminative, pixel-wise explanations.

## Key Claims
- **[ER-clrp-1]** Standard LRP explanations are **not class-discriminative**; CLRP produces instance-specific, class-discriminative, pixel-wise explanations via a contrastive signal.
  - Claim type: author claim · strength: supported
  - Evidence: abstract — "explanations generated by LRP are not class-discriminative … we propose Contrastive Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (CLRP)" (provenance: abstract)
  - Project relevance: justifies seeding the heatmap *direction* from a contrastive (margin) signal, not a single logit
- **[ER-clrp-2]** CLRP beats LRP qualitatively and quantitatively (Pointing Game + ablation).
  - Claim type: author claim · strength: supported
  - Evidence: abstract — "Both qualitative and quantitative evaluations show that the CLRP generates better explanations than the LRP" (provenance: abstract)

## Limitations / Open Questions
CNN-era method (not transformer/AttnLRP); abstract gives no numeric scores. We adopt the *contrastive idea*, applied to AttnLRP relevance over two class heads.
